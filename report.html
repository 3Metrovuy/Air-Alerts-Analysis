Air raid alert forecast, Ukraine

For each oblast and each 30-minute slot of the next 24 hours: the chance that an alert sounds at some point during that 30-minute slot. Even a 5-minute alert counts, so this is not the share of time under alert.

Data: volunteer (eTryvoga) alerts from the Vadimkin dataset, up to 2026-10-12 08:00 Kyiv. Test: the last 4 weeks (2026-09-14 .. 2026-10-12). A forecast is made every hour for every oblast; the model is retrained every week on the 6 months before that week, using only answers known by then. Generated by python main.py in 15 min.

How to read the scores

  • Right / wrong. The model "says alert" when its chance is 50% or more. The report counts how often that matched what happened, next to a lazy guess, the 28-day habit: say "alert" if this 30-minute slot of the day had an alert on most of the last 28 days. Without the lazy guess a high score misleads: in a quiet oblast, "never an alert" is right most of the time.
  • Alerts flagged: of the 30-minute slots that had an alert, the share where the model said "alert". Flags that came true: of the 30-minute slots where it said "alert", the share that had one. "—" means it never said "alert".
  • "When it says 70%": of all the times the model gave about 70%, how often an alert came. A good forecast gets close to 70%, like a weather forecast's chance of rain.
  • Brier skill: how much smaller the model's error is than the 28-day habit's error, where the error is (chance − outcome)² and the outcome is 1 or 0. 0% means no better; negative means worse.
  • ROC-AUC: take a random 30-minute slot with an alert and a random one without. How often does the model give the alert 30-minute slot the higher chance? 0.5 is a coin flip, 1.0 is perfect.

Why "no alert running" for the next 30-minute slot: the next 30-minute slot starts at the moment the forecast is made, so if an alert is already running it has an alert for certain. Counting those forecasts flatters any model (skill +40.9% with them, +10.6% without). The cards below only use the honest part.

All oblasts: how far ahead does it help?

Next 30-minute slot, no alert running: +10.6% Brier skill. From 2 h ahead: +1.0% to +1.7%, so beyond the next 30-minute slot the model is about as good as the oblast's 28-day rhythm. Alert history cannot see a specific attack coming hours ahead. ROC-AUC over all oblasts (0.73 at 3 h) is high mostly because the model tells busy oblasts from quiet ones; inside one oblast the ranking is weak (median 0.56 over the 24 oblasts at 3 h).

Checks

  • Leakage test: python test_leakage.py cuts the data at random moments, keeps only what was known then, rebuilds every input and asserts that nothing changes.
  • No training on the test: each weekly fold trains only on 30-minute slots whose answer was known before the fold starts.
  • The next-30-minute slot freebie is disclosed above: with an alert already running the skill is +97.0%, which is free by definition.
  • Robustness: the same pipeline on 12 earlier 4-week blocks (2025-10-13 .. 2026-09-14) gives +0.8% to +3.4% skill over all leads, positive in 12 of 12.

Data cleaning and limitations

  • Volunteer data means "eTryvoga reported an alert", not the official siren system; the two disagree in the west (Volyn, Lviv). Luhansk is dropped (3 alerts in 4 years); alerts under 1 minute are dropped.
  • Alerts longer than 12 h are missed all-clear messages: every one since Oct 2025 that could be checked against the official data was an artifact. Their slots get no label and no forecast whose last 24 h touches them. The rule uses hindsight but only removes rows.
