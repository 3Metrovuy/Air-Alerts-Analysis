Air raid alert forecast, Ukraine

For each oblast and each hour of the next 24 hours: the chance that an alert sounds at some point during that hour. Even a 5-minute alert counts, so this is not the share of time under alert.

Data: volunteer (eTryvoga) alerts from the Vadimkin dataset, up to 2026-10-12 08:00 Kyiv. Test: the last 4 weeks (2026-09-14 .. 2026-10-12). A forecast is made every hour for every oblast; the model is retrained every week on the 6 months before that week, using only answers known by then. Generated by python main.py in 9 min.

How to read the scores

  • Right / wrong. The model "says alert" when its chance is 50% or more. The report counts how often that matched what happened, next to a lazy guess, the 28-day habit: say "alert" if this hour of the day had an alert on most of the last 28 days. Without the lazy guess a high score misleads: in a quiet oblast, "never an alert" is right most of the time.
  • Alerts flagged: of the hours that had an alert, the share where the model said "alert". Flags that came true: of the hours where it said "alert", the share that had one. "—" means it never said "alert".
  • "When it says 70%": of all the times the model gave about 70%, how often an alert came. A good forecast gets close to 70%, like a weather forecast's chance of rain.
  • Brier skill: how much smaller the model's error is than the 28-day habit's error, where the error is (chance − outcome)² and the outcome is 1 or 0. 0% means no better; negative means worse.
  • ROC-AUC: take a random hour with an alert and a random one without. How often does the model give the alert hour the higher chance? 0.5 is a coin flip, 1.0 is perfect.

Why "no alert running" for the next hour: the next hour starts at the moment the forecast is made, so if an alert is already running it has an alert for certain. Counting those forecasts flatters any model (skill +25.5% with them, +4.8% without). The cards below only use the honest part.

All oblasts: how far ahead does it help?

Next hour, no alert running: +4.8% Brier skill. From 2 h ahead: +0.1% to +0.8%, so beyond the next hour the model is about as good as the oblast's 28-day rhythm. Alert history cannot see a specific attack coming hours ahead. ROC-AUC over all oblasts (0.76 at 3 h) is high mostly because the model tells busy oblasts from quiet ones; inside one oblast the ranking is weak (median 0.57 over the 24 oblasts at 3 h).

Checks

  • Leakage test: python test_leakage.py cuts the data at random moments, keeps only what was known then, rebuilds every input and asserts that nothing changes.
  • No training on the test: each weekly fold trains only on hours whose answer was known before the fold starts.
  • The next-hour freebie is disclosed above: with an alert already running the skill is +96.4%, which is free by definition.
  • Robustness: the same pipeline on 12 earlier 4-week blocks (2025-10-13 .. 2026-09-14) gives +0.9% to +3.4% skill over all leads, positive in 12 of 12.

Data cleaning and limitations

  • Volunteer data means "eTryvoga reported an alert", not the official siren system; the two disagree in the west (Volyn, Lviv). Luhansk is dropped (3 alerts in 4 years); alerts under 1 minute are dropped.
  • Alerts longer than 12 h are missed all-clear messages: every one since Oct 2025 that could be checked against the official data was an artifact. Their slots get no label and no forecast whose last 24 h touches them. The rule uses hindsight but only removes rows.
